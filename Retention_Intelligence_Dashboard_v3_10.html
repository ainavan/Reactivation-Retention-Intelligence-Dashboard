Initial operating targets: Contact Rate ≥ 15% (20% stretch); Active Rate ≥ 1% (1.5% stretch). Contact Rate = Contacted ÷ Assigned. Active Rate = Active Customers (Deposit + Trading) ÷ Assigned. These are proposed internal targets based on the October baseline and the previously stated 1% probation threshold, not universal industry benchmarks.

Proof Exceptions

Only records with an actual missing field, missing proof or confirmed mismatch appear in the correction list.

Audit policy: Contacted = 1 when ANY ONE of Call Data, Chatroom Data, or WhatsApp Data is 1. The Contacted record must also have Contacted Time and a proof photo on the same customer row. A 0 in one channel is not a conflict when another channel is 1. A placeholder name such as “--” is not treated as proof mismatch. OCR suggestions are advisory only; unreadable OCR does not create a correction item. Only structural issues or a supervisor-confirmed mismatch are included in the copy summary.
